Questions

Good to know

Do I need a kitchen?

No. The station runs on one power socket. Grill the sausage, warm the bread, add sauce. Anyone can learn it in 15 minutes.

Who sets the selling price?

You do. Most partners sell at 79–99 THB. You know your customers best.

What if I only sell a few?

At 79 THB, one hotdog every three days already covers the monthly fee. Everything above that is profit.

Can I stop?

Yes, any time with 30 days' notice. We pick up the station and there is nothing more to pay.

Who cleans the station?

Your staff, about 5 minutes at closing. We service and maintain the equipment.
