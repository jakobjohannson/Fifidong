Questions

Good to know

Do I need a kitchen?

No. Two small machines on your counter do the job: heat the sausage in the kettle, toast the baguette on the bread grill, add sauce, slide the sausage in. Anyone can learn it in 15 minutes.

Who sets the selling price?

You do. Most partners sell at 79–99 THB. You know your customers best.

What if I only sell a few?

At 79 THB, one hotdog every three days already covers the monthly fee. Everything above that is profit.

Can I stop?

Yes, any time with 30 days' notice. We pick up the machines and there is nothing more to pay.

Who cleans the machines?

Your staff, about 5 minutes at closing. We service and maintain the equipment.
